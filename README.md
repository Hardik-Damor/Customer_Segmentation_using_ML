# Customer_Segmentation_using_ML
 
## Problem Context

Our client is a UK-based **online retailer** that specializes in **all-occasion gifts**. While most of their customers are located in the UK, a **small portion** of their clientele is international.

To better serve these **international customers**, the company aims to **segment them into meaningful groups** based on their **previous purchase behavior**. This customer segmentation will help the business:

- Provide **tailored services** to different customer types
- **Personalize marketing strategies** for each group
- Improve overall **customer engagement and satisfaction**
